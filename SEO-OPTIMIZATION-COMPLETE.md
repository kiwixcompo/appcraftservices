## What Makes This "Natural"

### Context Over Keywords
Keywords appear because they're the natural way to describe the services, not because they're forced in.

### Varied Language
Using synonyms and related terms instead of repeating exact keyword phrases.

Keywords appear in context of explaining benefits and solving problems, not just listing features.
